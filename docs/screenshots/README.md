# Screenshots

Pictures for the README, the guides and the Nextcloud App Store. They were
taken in Nextcloud 33 with app version 0.1.26 and the public demo data from
[`tools/demo`](../../tools/demo/README.md): the invented choir "Demochor",
CC0. The accounts have neutral names ("Demo Librarian", "Demo Conductor",
"Demo Administrator"). The only real persons shown are composers, lyricists
and arrangers who died more than 70 years ago.

[`tools/screenshots`](../../tools/screenshots/README.md) recreates all of
them. Full-window pictures are 1600 × 1000 pixels; detail pictures are cut
to the part they show. Every file is a PNG with a palette of 256 colours.
