# For choir members

**English** | [Deutsch](de/fuer-chormitglieder.md)

One page to pass on: how to practise with the pieces the conductor shares
with the choir. You only need a browser, no app. The details are in the
[user guide](user-guide.md#as-a-choir-member).

## Signing in and finding the piece

1. Sign in with the choir's Nextcloud account and open **Cantoria**. A link
   in a message from the conductor opens the piece directly.
2. You see **Shared with me**: the pieces and excerpts for the choir, each
   with its validity. Choose one; the notes appear below (on a phone) or
   beside the list (on a large screen).
3. **Other pieces** below the title takes you back to the list.

## Practising your voice

1. In the bar above the notes, tap **Voice, measures and practising**.
2. Under **My voice**, choose your voice and **Focus voice louder** or
   **Focus voice only**.
3. Under **Measures and tempo**, enter **From measure** and **To measure**,
   tap **Show** and switch on **Loop**: the passage plays again and again.
   The tempo plays it slower.
4. **Play** starts; a tap on a note starts there.
5. **More for practising** has starting tones, metronome, lyrics, your own
   pitch and **Record myself** (use headphones).

## Practising without a network

Where it is switched on, switch on **Available without a network** at a
piece while you have a network. Then open the page as usual, also on the
train or in a church without Wi-Fi.

- **iPhone or iPad:** add the page to the home screen *first* (Share →
  **Add to Home Screen**), open it from there, sign in and then switch the
  pieces on. Safari and the home screen keep them apart.
- Open the page with a network at least once every 30 days, or the pieces
  are locked.
- On a shared device: **Delete everything on this device** at the bottom.

## If something does not work

| You see … | What helps |
|---|---|
| Cantoria is missing in Nextcloud, or "No access yet" | Ask the conductor whether your account is in the group of choir members. |
| "Nothing has been shared with you yet" | The conductor has not shared anything yet, or the link has expired. Ask for a new link. |
| No sound | Tap **Play** once (browsers play only after a tap) and check the silent switch of the phone. |
| "Locked: not checked with the server for more than 30 days" | Open the page once with a network. |
| Without a network the pieces are missing on the iPhone | You switched them on in Safari but open the home screen (or the other way round). Switch them on again where you practise. |
| "Show my pitch" or "Record myself" does not work | Allow the browser to use the microphone for this page. |

## Text for the conductor to send

> Hello everyone, the pieces for the next concert are in Cantoria under
> "Shared with me": [link]. Sign in with your choir account, choose your
> voice under "Voice, measures and practising" and practise difficult
> passages with "Loop". If you want to practise on the train, switch on
> "Available without a network" beforehand, with a network (on an iPhone,
> add the page to the home screen first). Questions welcome.
